I am a postdoctoral researcher at Amsterdam UMC in the [KIK department](https://kik.amsterdamumc.org/), where I work in collaboration with [Ameen Abu-Hanna](https://sites.google.com/view/homepage-aah/home) and [Giovanni Cina](https://sites.google.com/site/homepagegcina/). My current research focuses on sequential decision-making and causal inference.


I hold a [Ph.D. in Statistics](https://theses.hal.science/tel-04770912/), completed under the supervision of [Stanley Durrleman](https://who.rocq.inria.fr/Stanley.Durrleman/) and [Sophie Tezenas Du Montcel](https://www.researchgate.net/profile/Sophie-Tezenas-Du-Montcel) within the INRIA ([ARAMIS](https://www.aramislab.fr/) team) and supported by the [PRAIRIE institute](https://www.prairie-psai.fr/research-and-innovation/community/ortholand-juliette/). During my doctoral research, I developed statistical methods for the joint modelling of longitudinal outcomes and censoring events, including [new approach in this area](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/10.1177/09622802251399917?url_ver=Z39.88-2003&rfr_id=ori:rid:crossref.org&rfr_dat=cr_pub%20%200pubmed).  
I then completed a first postdoctoral position in collaboration with [Marie-Abèle Bind](https://biostatistics.massgeneral.org/faculty/marie-abele-bind-phd/) (Harvard Medical School and MGH Biostatistics) and [Young Lee](https://www.sutd.edu.sg/profile/young-lee) (Singapore University of Technology and Design), where I studied causal questions arising in longitudinal data truncated by death.

While my primary interest lies in methodological research, I place strong emphasis on ensuring that new methods are motivated by and useful for real clinical questions.
